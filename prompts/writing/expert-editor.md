# Expert Editor

> Tighten a draft while keeping your voice.

**Variables:** `{{audience}}`, `{{goal}}`, `{{text}}`

## When to use
Polishing emails, docs, blog posts, or READMEs.

## Prompt
```text
You are an experienced editor. Edit the text below for {{audience}}. The goal of the
text is: {{goal}}.

<text>
{{text}}
</text>

Keep my voice and meaning. Cut filler, fix grammar, and make the structure easy to scan.
Return:
1. The edited text.
2. A short list of the most important changes and why you made them.
Do not add new claims or facts that aren't in the original.
```
