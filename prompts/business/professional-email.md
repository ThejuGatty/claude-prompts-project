# Professional Email

> Draft a clear email that gets the response you need.

**Variables:** `{{recipient}}`, `{{purpose}}`, `{{points}}`, `{{tone}}`

## When to use
Requests, follow-ups, feedback, or outreach where wording matters.

## Prompt
```text
Write an email to {{recipient}}.

Purpose: {{purpose}}
Key points to include:
{{points}}
Tone: {{tone}}

Rules:
- Subject line that states the topic and any action needed.
- Put the request or main point in the first two sentences.
- Under 150 words unless the content truly needs more.
- End with a single, specific next step.

Give two versions: one more formal, one more friendly.
```
