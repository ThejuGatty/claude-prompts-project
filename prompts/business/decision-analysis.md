# Analyze a Decision

> Look at a decision from several angles before committing.

**Variables:** `{{decision}}`, `{{options}}`, `{{constraints}}`

## When to use
Career moves, technology choices, purchases, or any decision with trade-offs.

## Prompt
```text
Help me decide: {{decision}}

Options I'm considering:
{{options}}

Constraints and priorities:
{{constraints}}

For each option give the main benefits, risks, and what would have to be true for it
to be the best choice. Then argue against the option I seem to favor. Finally, give
your recommendation, how confident you are, and what information would change it.
```
